PicoZorro One TH rev A - order options
- 2 layers, FR-4, 1.6 mm, 155.5 x 65.92 mm (tongue included)
- edge connector (Zorro II, 2 x 50 fingers at 2.54 mm, hardware/EDGE_CONNECTOR.md): gold fingers,
  0.5 x 45 degree bevel on the finger edge; finish ENIG recommended (README.md, Mechanical), HASL works
- the finger window is one open solder-mask area by design
- vias tented

One board, two builds. Fit one of them, never both parts of a pair (they share the footprint area).

DIY build (no assembly order; parts list picozorro-one-th-parts.csv, the rows without DNP)
  The builder solders everything, all through-hole:
  - D1 1N5817, F1 PTC 0.9 A, C1 100 uF, C2 220 uF, Q1 2N3904, R1 2.2 k
  - U2 LD1117V33 (TO-220, lying, 3V3_AUX regulator), C3 10 uF 25 V electrolytic (U2 input, mind the +),
    C4 10 uF (U2 output)
  - J2 USB-A (or J3, the 2x5 header)
  - U1 Core2350B on P1-P4, M1 W5500 Lite on M1J1 / M1J2; optional M2 microSD breakout, M3 GY-PCM5102 on
    M3J1 / M3J2
  - JP2 stays open (as delivered): U2 supplies 3V3_AUX. Bridge JP2 only on a board without U2 (and never
    with U2 fitted): the module's 3V3 then feeds M1 / M2
  - optional: J4 SWD, J5 RUN/BOOT; JP1 recut for /INT6
  - not fitted: the SMD footprints (D101, R101, F101, C301, Q101, U201, R6, C401, C101-C103, C201-C205, J6,
    R2-R5, C5, C6)

JLC build (PCBA order: jlc/picozorro-one-th-bom-jlc.csv + jlc/picozorro-one-th-cpl-jlc.csv, top side)
  JLC places the SMD set (KiCad variant JLC). Check the part rotations in JLC's DFM preview: the CPL carries
  the corrections of kicad-jlcpcb-tools for SOT-223 (+180) and SOT-23 (-90), none for the others.
  The builder then solders by hand, all through-hole:
  - the modules on their headers: U1 Core2350B (P1-P4), M1 W5500 Lite (M1J1 / M1J2), optional M3 GY-PCM5102
    (M3J1 / M3J2)
  - J2 USB-A (or J3, the 2x5 header)
  - nothing else: JP2 stays open (U201 AMS1117-3.3 with C301 / R6 / C401, fitted by JLC, supplies 3V3_AUX); no M2 (J6 is the microSD socket); the through-hole
    alternates (D1, R1, F1, C1, C2, C3, C4, Q1, U2, M2) stay empty; optional J4 / J5 as in the DIY build
  - Economic PCBA does not offer gold fingers: ENIG without bevel there, or
    Standard PCBA for hard-gold bevelled fingers
